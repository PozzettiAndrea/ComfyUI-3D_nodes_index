[Ryuukeisyou/comfyui_io_helpers](https://github.com/Ryuukeisyou/comfyui_io_helpers) (Image to base64)
[glowcone/comfyui-base64-to-image](https://github.com/glowcone/comfyui-base64-to-image) (Base64 to image)
