# ComfyUI Droopy Noodles

A ComfyUI extension that makes your node connections delightfully droopy. (Disclaimer: despite what it may look like, this extension will *not* make your monitor taste like spaghetti.)

## What Makes Your Noodles Droopy

- **Gravity-affected noodles**: Your connections succumb to the inexorable pull of gravity
- **Collision detection**: Your noodles rest leisurely on nodes instead of rudely phasing through them like rude ghosts
- **Buttery smooth wiggling**: Oodles of fps of pure noodle physics for maximum satisfaction
- **Universal noodle compatibility**: Works with all ComfyUI connection types - IMAGE noodles, LATENT noodles, even CONDITIONING noodles!
- **Flow animation support**: Watch data flow through your droopy noodles like sauce through pasta

## How to Install Your Noodle Droopifier

1. Navigate to your ComfyUI custom_nodes directory
2. Clone or copy this extension into the directory
3. Restart ComfyUI and prepare for maximum droop

## Making Things Droopy

1. After installation, open ComfyUI
2. Open Settings
3. Navigate to the "Lite Graph" category
4. Select "Droopy" as your "Link Render Mode"
5. Enjoy

## Noodle Tuning Parameters

You can fine-tune your noodle droopiness with scientifically-proven parameters in the "DroopyNoodles" category of Settings.
